InTempo

Lessons, students and invoices for music teachers, all in one place.

What InTempo does

InTempo is a desktop app for independent music teachers. It keeps a teacher’s lesson schedule, student details and invoices together, so less time goes on paperwork and more on teaching.

  • LessonsBook, move and cancel one-off lessons in a simple weekly view.
  • StudentsKeep each student’s and parent or guardian’s contact details.
  • InvoicesCreate invoices from booked lessons, then save them as PDF or print them.
  • Google CalendarOptionally keep lessons in step with the teacher’s own Google Calendar.

Your information stays on your computer

InTempo stores its information on the teacher’s own computer. There is no InTempo account and no InTempo server. Signing in with Google is used to identify the teacher and, when calendar sync is turned on, to show and update their lessons in Google Calendar. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
