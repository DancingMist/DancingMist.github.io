InTempo

Invoicing made easy for freelance music teachers, so you’re paid right for every lesson.

What InTempo does

InTempo is a desktop app for freelance music teachers. Most teachers were never taught the business side of teaching: sending invoices, keeping track of who has paid, and keeping their finances in order. InTempo takes care of that paperwork and guides you through it, so less time goes on admin and you’re paid correctly.

It works alongside the tools you already use. There’s no need to move your teaching into a new app: keep booking lessons in your own calendar, keep talking to parents the way you do now, and let InTempo do the paperwork.

  • InvoicesCreate invoices from your lessons in your own layout, then save them as PDF or print them.
  • Getting paidSee who has paid and who is overdue, and handle cancellations and credits consistently.
  • StudentsKeep each student’s and parent or guardian’s contact details together.

Works with your Google Calendar, both ways

InTempo connects to the Google Calendar you already use and keeps your lessons in sync in both directions. Add, move or cancel a lesson in Google Calendar and InTempo updates to match; make a change in InTempo and it appears in your calendar. Your lessons are read from your calendar, however you’ve titled them, so you can carry on booking the way you always have.

Calendar sync is optional and you turn it on yourself. InTempo only ever changes the lesson events it looks after. Your personal events are never changed.

Your information stays on your computer

InTempo stores its information on the teacher’s own computer. There is no InTempo account and no InTempo server. Signing in with Google is used to identify the teacher and, when calendar sync is turned on, to show and update their lessons in Google Calendar. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
