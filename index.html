InTempo

Invoicing & Booking Made Easy for Freelance Music Teachers

What Is InTempo?

InTempo is a desktop app built for freelance music teachers. It manages the business side of teaching: invoicing, tracking payments and keeping your finances in order. You spend less time on admin, and you’re paid correctly for every lesson.

Teaching is your expertise; invoices, payments and records often aren’t. InTempo takes care of them for you.

InTempo works alongside the tools you already use rather than replacing them. Keep scheduling lessons in your own calendar and keep in touch with parents the way you do now. InTempo takes care of the paperwork.

  • InvoicesCreate invoices from your lessons in your own layout, then save them as PDF or print them.
  • Getting PaidSee who has paid and who is overdue, and handle cancellations and credits consistently.
  • Cash or Bank TransferRecord how and when each invoice was paid, so your records are right at tax time.
  • StudentsKeep each student’s and parent or guardian’s contact details together.
  • Lesson Notes for ParentsJot down what you covered and what to practise, then copy a ready-made message into KakaoTalk, WeChat, a text or an email. Reminders make sure no lesson is missed.

Two-Way Sync with Google Calendar

InTempo connects to your existing Google Calendar and keeps your lessons in sync in both directions. When you add, move or cancel a lesson in Google Calendar, InTempo updates to match, and changes made in InTempo appear in your calendar. Lessons are recognised however you title them, so there’s no need to change the way you schedule.

Calendar sync is optional, and only you can turn it on. InTempo only changes the lesson events it manages. Your personal events are never changed.

What’s Next for InTempo

These features are in development. Plans may change as we hear from teachers.

  • Tax Time ReportsAt the end of the financial year, one click gives you your income by month and by student, cash and bank transfer, ready for your accountant or tax return.
  • Email Lesson NotesSend lesson notes to parents straight from your own Gmail, so they come from you, not an app.
  • GST and ABNTax invoices that meet the ATO’s rules, for teachers registered for GST.
  • Automatic BackupsYour information saved automatically to a folder you choose, such as OneDrive.
  • Your AvailabilitySet the times you’re available to teach each day, and see your whole calendar inside InTempo.
  • On Your PhoneInTempo on Android and iPhone, with the same information as your computer.
  • Parent LinksA private link for each family, with no sign-up. Parents see their child’s lessons and your availability, read their lesson notes, and can send you a message about a note straight to your inbox.

Your Information Stays on Your Computer

InTempo stores its information on the teacher’s own computer. There is no InTempo account and no InTempo server. Signing in with Google is used to identify the teacher and, when calendar sync is turned on, to show and update their lessons in Google Calendar. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
