Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 (calendar sync added)

This policy explains how the InTempo desktop app (“InTempo”, “the app”) handles information, including information from your Google account. InTempo is developed by Kevin Leong in Australia. If you have any questions, email kleong.jkim@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Everything you enter in InTempo is stored on your own computer.
  • There is no InTempo server, and we never receive a copy of your information.
  • Google sign-in is used only to identify you and, if you turn on calendar sync, to show and update your lessons in Google Calendar.
  • We don’t sell, share or use your information for advertising.

Information you enter in the app

InTempo stores the information you type into it: your business details (such as your name, address, contact and bank details, and logo), your students and their parents’ or guardians’ names and contact details, your lessons, rates and invoices. This information is saved in a file on your computer. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It only leaves your computer if you choose to, for example by saving a backup, saving an invoice as a PDF, printing, or emailing an invoice yourself.

Information from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, InTempo receives:

  • Your name and email address, used to show who is signed in, for example on the welcome screen.
  • If you turn on calendar sync: access to the events on your main Google Calendar. InTempo uses this only to keep your lessons and your calendar in step:
    • lessons you add, change, cancel or delete in InTempo are added, changed or removed as calendar events titled like “Amy Chen Lesson”;
    • events with the word “Lesson” in their title (for example “Amy Chen Lesson (Online)”) are imported as lessons, creating the student in InTempo if needed, and upcoming ones get a hidden InTempo label so the app can recognise them;
    • when a lesson event is moved or deleted in Google Calendar, the lesson is updated or removed in InTempo.
    InTempo reads your other events only to find lesson events. It never changes them and doesn’t keep a copy of them.

This information is used only to provide these features to you. It is kept on your computer. It is not transferred to us or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models.

InTempo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How your sign-in is kept secure

Signing in happens on Google’s own sign-in page in your web browser. InTempo never sees your Google password. The access Google grants to InTempo is stored on your computer, encrypted using your computer’s built-in security.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Information from Google is exchanged directly between your computer and Google. InTempo has no server of its own, so there is no InTempo copy of your data.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Signing out in InTempo (Settings → Sign Out) removes the saved sign-in from your computer and asks Google to cancel InTempo’s access.
  • You can also remove InTempo’s access at any time from your Google Account’s third-party access page.
  • Your InTempo information stays on your computer until you delete it. Uninstalling the app keeps your information in case you reinstall. To remove it completely, delete the InTempo folder in your computer’s app data folder (on Windows, %APPDATA%\InTempo) and any backup files you saved.

Children’s information

InTempo is used by teachers, not by students. Teachers may enter their students’ names and their parents’ or guardians’ contact details. This information stays on the teacher’s computer and is not sent to us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Contact
