Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026 (Parent Links; also emailing invoices and lesson notes, PDF invoices, ABN check, expenses and tax reports, calendar view and availability)

This policy explains how InTempo handles information, including information from your Google account. InTempo is available as a web app that you use in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone (“the web version”), and as a Windows desktop app (“the desktop app”). InTempo is developed by Kevin Leong in Australia. If you have any questions, email kleong.jkim@gmail.com.

The short version

  • In the web version, your information is stored securely online, in a private space that only your signed-in Google account can open.
  • In the desktop app, your information is stored on your own computer.
  • Google sign-in is used only to identify you and, if you turn on calendar sync, to show and update your lessons in Google Calendar.
  • We don’t sell, share or use your information for advertising.

Information you enter in the app

InTempo stores the information you type into it: your business details (such as your name, address, contact and bank details, and logo), your students and their parents’ or guardians’ names and contact details, your lessons, rates and invoices, the work expenses you record, the hours you’re available to teach, and any lesson notes and private notes you write about your students.

  • Web version: this information is stored on servers run by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, in a separate database for each teacher’s account. Only your signed-in account can reach your database. Cloudflare stores it on our behalf and may store it outside Australia. We don’t look at your information, except when you ask us to help with a problem or where the law requires it.
    Cloudflare also keeps backup copies of each database for 30 days. We use them only to recover your information, for example if something is deleted by mistake and you ask us to put it back. We also keep a short list of the teachers using the web version (name, email address and when the app was last used), so we can find your account when you ask for help or for it to be deleted.
  • Desktop app: this information is saved in a file on your computer. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Your information only goes anywhere else if you choose to send it, for example by downloading your data, emailing an invoice from InTempo, saving an invoice as a PDF, printing, emailing an invoice yourself, or copying a lesson note to send to a parent.

Information from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, InTempo receives:

  • Your name and email address, used to show who is signed in, for example on the welcome screen.
  • If you turn on calendar sync: access to the events on your main Google Calendar. InTempo uses this only to keep your lessons and your calendar in step:
    • lessons you add, change, cancel or delete in InTempo are added, changed or removed as calendar events titled like “Amy Chen Lesson”;
    • events whose title marks them as a lesson (for example “Amy Chen Lesson (Online)” or “Trial - Amy Chen”; you can change these words in Settings) or names one of your students are imported as lessons, from four weeks before the week you turn sync on onwards. The student is created in InTempo if needed, and upcoming lessons get a hidden InTempo label so the app can recognise them;
    • when a lesson event is moved or deleted in Google Calendar, the lesson is updated or removed in InTempo.
    InTempo reads your other events only to find lesson events, to show them (greyed out) in its calendar view, and to work out your free time from the hours you’re available. It never changes them and doesn’t keep a copy of them.
  • If you email an invoice or lesson notes from InTempo (web version): permission to send email from your Gmail. InTempo uses it only to send the invoices and lesson notes you choose to email, from your own address (invoices with the invoice attached as a PDF). It can’t read, change or delete your email. A copy appears in your Gmail’s Sent folder, and InTempo keeps a note of when and to whom each one was emailed. If you use Parent Links, a message a parent sends you through their link is also emailed to your own inbox from your Gmail, with replies going to the parent.

This information is used only to provide these features to you. It is not transferred to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models.

InTempo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Parent Links (web version)

A teacher can turn on a private link for a student’s family. It needs no sign-up, and the teacher can turn it off or replace it at any time, after which the old link stops working. The page asks search engines not to list it.

  • What the family sees: that student’s own lessons, when the teacher has time free (shown either as broad parts of the day or as exact times, as the teacher chooses), and the lesson notes the teacher has sent for that student. Other students and the teacher’s own events only ever appear as busy, never with names or details. Families can’t book or change anything.
  • Working out free time: when a family opens the page, InTempo’s server reads the start and end times of the teacher’s other Google Calendar events (not their titles or details) to know when the teacher is busy. Nothing from the calendar is kept.
  • Messages: a family can send the teacher a message about a lesson note. The message is kept in the teacher’s InTempo account and emailed to the teacher (see above). The reply address is the parent’s email from the student’s details, or one the parent types in, and is used only so the teacher can reply. A link can send only a few messages a day.
  • The family’s page sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics.

How your sign-in is kept secure

Signing in happens on Google’s own sign-in page. InTempo never sees your Google password.

  • Web version: sign-in is completed on InTempo’s server. The long-lasting access Google grants to InTempo is kept on the server and is never sent to your browser. When calendar sync is on, your browser is given a short-lived key (lasting about an hour) so it can update your calendar directly with Google. Your browser is also given a secure sign-in cookie, which scripts can’t read, that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.
  • Desktop app: the access Google grants to InTempo is stored on your computer, encrypted using your computer’s built-in security.

Cookies and storage on your device

The web version uses one cookie to keep you signed in, plus a short-lived one (about 10 minutes) while you sign in. It also remembers on your device which reminders it has already shown you. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The only other company involved is Cloudflare, which hosts the web version and stores its information on our behalf. Cloudflare also turns invoices and tax reports into PDF files when you save or email one; nothing is kept after the PDF is made. When you enter an ABN in the web version, it is checked against the Australian Business Register’s public ABN Lookup service, to confirm it’s active and whether it’s registered for GST. Information from Google is exchanged directly between InTempo and Google.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Signing out (Settings → Sign Out) signs you out. In the web version it signs you out on all your devices. In the desktop app it removes the saved sign-in from your computer and asks Google to cancel InTempo’s access.
  • You can remove InTempo’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account’s third-party access page.
  • Web version: you can download a copy of everything at any time (Settings → Backup → Download My Data), and load a copy back in with Restore from File.
  • Web version: your information is kept until you ask us to delete it. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email kleong.jkim@gmail.com from the Google account you use with InTempo, and we will delete it within 30 days and cancel InTempo’s access to your Google account. Cloudflare’s backup copies are removed automatically within 30 days after that.
  • Desktop app: your information stays on your computer until you delete it. Uninstalling the app keeps your information in case you reinstall. To remove it completely, delete the InTempo folder in your computer’s app data folder (on Windows, %APPDATA%\InTempo) and any backup files you saved.

Children’s information

InTempo is used by teachers, not by students. Teachers may enter their students’ names, their parents’ or guardians’ contact details, and notes about their lessons (for example what was covered and what to practice). This information is kept in the teacher’s own private space (online in the web version, or on their computer in the desktop app), and is not shared with anyone. Lesson notes reach parents only when the teacher copies and sends them, emails them from their own Gmail, or turns on a Parent Link for that student’s family.

Changes to this policy
